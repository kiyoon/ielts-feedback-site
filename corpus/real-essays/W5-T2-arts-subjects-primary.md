# W5 (Task 2) — Music, art and drama at primary level (real student essay)

**Source:** student-written practice essay (pasted into Claude Code session, 2026-08-24).
**Task type:** IELTS Writing Task 2 agree/disagree essay.
**Prompt source:** Included with the pasted essay.

## Prompt

Some say that music, art and drama are as important as other school subjects, especially at the primary level. Do you agree or disagree?

## Essay

It is often argued that especially for elementary school students, minor subjects such as music, art or drama are as crucial as core subjects. This essay totally agree with that statement because they can encourage students to develop their potential talents in the early stage and also help them improve their creativity.

The primary reason why the school should treat minor subjects as important as other subjects is because everyone has their own unique talents, and it is crucial to find those unique specialities as soon as possible, even though it can be difficult to find out which subject will be the one. Moreover, it is highly likely that the students are going to focus more on major subjects when they become high school students as they will spend more time on preparing university's entrance exam, which will lead them to lose the opportunity to study those minor subjects and eventually, cannot develop their talents. For example, a lot of K-POP artists need to be trained when they are teenager, and figuring out their skills as soon as possible can lead to success.

The second main reason why teaching those minor subjects is important especially for elementary school students is that they can help students think in more creative ways. Most of the major subjects such as mathematics or science have a specific answer, whereas the subjects like music, art and drama do not. This will help the pupils think in more unconventional ways and foster their creativity. For instance, it is possible to draw a person in various ways, and there are millions of unique mdelodies and scenarios.

In conclusion, I strongly believe that subjects like music, art and drama are equally important as other major subjects since they can develop the children's potential talents and boost their creativity.
