## Prompt

Some people say that the best way to improve public health is by increasing the number of
sports facilities. Others, however, say that this would have little effect on public health and that other measures are required.

Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

## Model answer (Band 7)

Some people regard building more sports facilities as the best way to improve public health, while others believe that different measures are needed. Although nearby facilities can encourage regular exercise, I believe they are insufficient on their own because people's health also depends on their everyday habits, including diet.

Supporters of additional facilities argue that convenient access makes it easier to stay active. When the nearest swimming pool or sports centre requires a long journey, residents may struggle to fit exercise around work and family responsibilities. Consider a resident in Korea who has stopped swimming because the nearest pool is too far from home. If a new pool opened nearby, this person might return regularly, gradually improving their fitness. By reducing the time and effort needed to participate, local facilities can help people maintain an exercise routine. This gives supporters a practical reason to prioritise investment in them.

However, easier access may have little effect on people who are not interested in sport, and it does not address unhealthy eating habits. Other measures are therefore needed to improve health more widely. Schools could provide nutrition lessons that teach children how to choose balanced meals and replace sugary drinks with water. Offering healthier lunches would support these lessons by making nutritious food available during the school day. Such measures could improve pupils' diets regardless of whether they enjoy competitive sports. In my view, combining education with practical support offers a stronger response than relying on additional buildings alone.

In conclusion, more local sports facilities can remove a barrier to regular exercise and improve fitness. Nevertheless, I believe they should form part of a broader approach that also helps people develop healthier eating habits.
