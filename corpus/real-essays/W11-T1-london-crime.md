## Prompt

The line graph below shows the number of serious crimes that occurred in London between 2003 and 2012.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

## Prompt image

real-essays/raw-materials/W11-T1-london-crime.png

## Essay

The provided line graph displays how the number of major crimes occurred in London changed from 2003 to 2012.

Overall, the number of armed robbery showed a plummet during the given time. On the contrary, a slight increase can be seen in the number of assault cases while the number of murder remained stable.

In detail, the figure for armed robbery began at just below 3,500 cases in 2003. Then, it rose by approximately 250 cases in the following year, and started to decrease dramatically to just over 1,000 cases between 2004 and 2008. After the mere increase of roughly 500 cases in 2009, the figure maintained the similar level at around 1,500 cases until 2012.

Turning to the figure for Assault, it started at about 2,300 cases. After that, a decline of around 300 cases can be seen from 2003 to 2008. Afterwards, there was a gradual climb of approximately 800 cases between 2008 and 2012, eventually reached a peak during the given period. Meanwhile, the figure for murder stayed relatively stable from 2003 to 2012, maintained the similar level with merely 500 cases.
