## Prompt

Many people are now opting to provide technology companies with their personal data in exchange for access to software.

Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

## Model answer (Band 7)

In recent years, a growing number of people have chosen to give technology companies their personal information in return for access to software. Although this exchange exposes users to the risk of data theft, I believe its benefits outweigh its drawbacks, because the services it unlocks save people considerable time and money.

Admittedly, the danger is real. Criminals attempt to breach corporate databases in order to steal personal information, because such data can be sold at a substantial profit. In Korea, for example, hackers frequently target technology firms rather than banks or government agencies, since these firms hold vast amounts of user data and their security is often easier to breach. However, declining a single service does not make anyone safe: much personal information is already held in digital form by employers, hospitals and banks. Handing data to one more company adds one more point of exposure — a real cost, but a marginal one.

The principal advantage of this exchange is what the data actually buys. Since the advent of artificial intelligence, ordinary users have been able to obtain basic health or legal guidance from AI chatbot services that would cost a substantial sum if they consulted a doctor or a lawyer. Some AI agents will even build a customised program on request. Convenient as these tools are, they are not gifts: the access is offered in exchange for the personal data users provide. For most people the trade is worth making: the savings are immediate and certain, whereas the risk of theft remains only a possibility.

In conclusion, although sharing personal data with technology companies does create a genuine risk of theft, that risk is both modest and largely unavoidable in a digital society. The convenience and savings that cutting-edge software delivers outweigh it, which is why so many users are willing to make the trade.
