## Prompt

Nowadays, more tasks at home and work are being performed by robots.

Why do you think this is happening?

Is it a positive or negative development?

## Essay

Robots are handling more tasks that humans used to do at work and their home. This is because using robots is relatively more efficient than doing all tasks by people, and I believe this is a positive development because they can help us focus more on bigger projects.

Finishing a lot of tasks with the help of robots can be very efficient. The reason is because robots such as computers, smartphones or other devices are better at performing repetitive works with their fast calculation speed. Furthermore, they do not need to take a rest unlike humans. For example, AI agents developed by OpenAI are able to create simple software such as web pages or video games like Tetris; and AI agents are willing to find bugs without hesitation even at midnight.

Utilizing robots should be welcomed because, when they handle small errands for people, individuals can work on more important tasks. Humans have limitations in terms of willpower and stamina since they are organic creatures. Therefore, people cannot focus on everything around them and have to choose which tasks they should focus on. If robots can handle small things for people, they can solely focus on managing all aspects of the project they work on. For instance, people used to do coding everything by themselves, including the shape of buttons. However, nowadays, people only need to care for the content of their program since AI can work on small elements.

In conclusion, I believe this is a positive trend because robots can help people work on bigger projects more efficiently.
