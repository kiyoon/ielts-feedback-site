## Prompt

Nowadays, more tasks at home and work are being performed by robots.

Why do you think this is happening?

Is it a positive or negative development?

## Model answer (Band 7)

Robots are taking over a growing number of tasks in homes and workplaces. This is largely because they can perform routine work efficiently and consistently. I consider this a positive development because delegating repetitive tasks gives people more time and energy for responsibilities that require personal attention.

One reason for the increasing use of robots is their ability to repeat the same operation without becoming tired. In a factory, for example, a robotic arm could fit components together throughout a night shift, while employees supervise the process and check the finished products. Consistent output makes production easier to plan and gives employers a practical reason to automate suitable tasks. A similar principle applies at home, where a robotic vacuum can clean a floor with limited input from its owner. Although these machines still need maintenance and supervision, they can reduce the effort involved in routine work.

I welcome this change because human time and concentration are limited. When workers spend less energy on repetitive physical tasks, they can devote more attention to planning projects, solving unexpected problems and improving quality. Automation therefore changes how their effort is used, rather than simply making the same job faster. The benefit can also be seen in a household: a parent could let a robotic vacuum clean the floor while helping a child with homework. The cleaning still gets done, but the parent can concentrate on a responsibility that needs direct involvement. Such delegation can make daily life more manageable.

In conclusion, robots are increasingly adopted because they can carry out routine tasks efficiently. I regard this as beneficial because the time and energy saved allow people to give greater attention to other responsibilities at work and at home.
