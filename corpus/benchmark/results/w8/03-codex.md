## Structural feedback

You answer both questions and use the correct four-paragraph problem–solution structure. The introduction lists fossil fuels before accidents, whereas Body 1 reverses that order; matching them would make the roadmap cleaner. The accident argument is circular—more cars produce more traffic, so accidents rise “naturally”—and misses the prompt-specific mechanism of inexperienced first-time drivers. Body 2 appropriately offers one solution, but three sentences repeat *fewer private cars*, while the campaign lacks a reason people would actually change transport. Fewer cars would implicitly reduce both accidents and fuel demand, but that two-part link should be stated. The single highest-impact fix is rebuilding Body 2 around affordable, reliable public transport → changed behaviour → fewer accidents and lower fuel demand, not the lexical polishing below.
