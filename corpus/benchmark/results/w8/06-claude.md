## Top concrete rewrites (ranked by band uplift × persistence)

1. **Give the solution a mechanism.**
   Before: "One simple action that governments can do is conduct a campaign to make people use public transports more than usual."
   After: "Governments can run awareness campaigns, but the decisive step is to make public transport the cheaper and easier choice — subsidising fares and expanding bus and subway networks so a private car is no longer the only convenient option."
   Why: campaign-only solutions are the classic under-developed policy paragraph (`task-response-upgrades.md` §8.4; policy verbs in `contexts/09-instructor-tips/09-topic-vocabulary-clusters.md` §13). Lifts TR.

2. **Prove the accident claim with the prompt's own ingredients.**
   Before: "This is because if people buy more cars, there will be more traffic, and thus, car accidents will increase naturally."
   After: "Millions of these buyers are first-time drivers with little experience, and licensing and enforcement in many developing countries are too weak to prepare them, so collision rates rise faster than car ownership itself."
   Why: replaces circular assertion with a mechanism only this prompt allows (`task-response-upgrades.md` §§5–6; `band-6-common-errors.md` §D7). Lifts TR.

3. **"reusable energy" → "renewable energy"** ("unlike renewable sources such as solar and wind power"). Third appearance of this collocation family since W2, where feedback corrected it explicitly (`topic-02-environment.md`). Lifts LR.

4. **Countability sweep.** "there will be less cars on roads" → "there would be **fewer cars on the roads**"; "less traffics on road" → "**less traffic on the roads**"; "public transports" → "**public transport**" (`band-6-common-errors.md` §A6; `02-vocabulary-error-correction-bank.md` §9). Lifts GRA and LR at once — your longest-running error family.

5. **Un-splice the thesis.** "…are more consumption of fossil fuels and more car accidents**. Nevertheless,** these issues can be tackled…" — full stop (or semicolon) before *nevertheless* (`10-common-korean-chinese-speaker-errors.md` §I1; `contexts/08-vocabulary-grammar/05-spelling-and-mechanics.md` §2).

6. **Make one sentence serve both problems.**
   Before: "a single vehicle can handle several people at once, instead of people driving their own vehicles each."
   After: "a single bus can carry dozens of passengers who would otherwise drive, which reduces both collision risk and fuel consumption."
   Why: fixes the collocation *and* states the fossil-fuel link the essay leaves implicit (`contexts/09-instructor-tips/06-task2-sentence-frames.md` §4; `task-response-upgrades.md` §8.4).

7. **Repair the conclusion's splice and pronoun in one edit.**
   Before: "…more car accidents and increasing fossil fuel usage, however, it can be alleviated through conducting a public awareness campaign."
   After: "…more road accidents and greater fossil fuel consumption**. However, both problems** can be eased by making public transport a genuinely attractive alternative."
   Why: kills splice #2, gives the pronoun a visible referent (`03-cohesion-error-correction-bank.md` §3), and stops the conclusion shrinking your solution.

8. **Drop the reflexive.** "fossil fuels take millions of years to form themselves naturally" → "fossil fuels take millions of years to **form**, so once burned they cannot be replaced." Tightens register and adds the consequence clause Band 7 rewards.
