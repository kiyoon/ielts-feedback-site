## Structural feedback

First, both topic sentences are formulaic 30–40-word chains ("The primary reason why the school should treat minor subjects as important as other subjects is because…"; "The second main reason why teaching those minor subjects is important especially for elementary school students is that…") — shorter claims would sharpen progression. Third, the conclusion restates rather than synthesises — the recurring W2/W4 pattern (`contexts/05-band-6-to-7/task-response-upgrades.md` §9).
