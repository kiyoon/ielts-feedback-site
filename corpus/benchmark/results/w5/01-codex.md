## Structural feedback

All parts of the prompt are addressed, and the agreement position remains clear from introduction to conclusion. The two body paragraphs have distinct controlling ideas—early talent development and creativity—but Body 1 is considerably longer than Body 2. Body 1 explains later exam pressure well, although its K-POP example concerns teenage training rather than showing what primary-school lessons provide. Body 2 follows a basic PEEL sequence, but its final illustration merely lists possible drawings, melodies and scenarios without explaining how this creativity benefits wider learning. The conclusion repeats the roadmap rather than synthesising the educational value of both benefits. The highest-impact structural fix is to turn Body 2’s illustration into an explained claim about how creative problem-solving transfers across the curriculum; this matters more to argument quality than adding rarer vocabulary.
