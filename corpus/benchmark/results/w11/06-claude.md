**Lexical Resource 6.** Range is real but partner-word control is not consistent: "showed a plummet" (noun not in the §7 bank), "the mere increase" (*mere* + change noun is not an English collocation; §11 gives *modest*), "maintained the similar level" (article), "with merely 500 cases" (preposition). Meaning is always clear, which is Band 6, not Band 5.

**Grammatical Range & Accuracy 6.** You control passives, relative-free reduced clauses, prepositional phrases and time adverbials. Errors persist in complex sentences: two finite verbs after a comma with no subject ("eventually reached a peak"; ", maintained the similar level"), and "how the number of major crimes **occurred** in London changed" needs *occurring* or *that occurred* — a second finite verb inside a clause that already has one (`contexts/09-instructor-tips/10-common-korean-chinese-speaker-errors.md` §F1). Band 7 wants error-free sentences to be *frequent*; here most long sentences carry one.
