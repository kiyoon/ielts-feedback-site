## Structural feedback

Your introduction does the hardest thing right, and it is the exact thing W3 got wrong: it paraphrases both views and lands on the prompt's own terms. The essay then never comes back to that position. Your conclusion concedes the teacher case and trails into a subordinate observation — a reader finishing there could not say which side you took, which is the textbook Band 6 marker "the conclusions drawn may be unclear". The length is fine at 33 words; the problem is that all 33 are spent on the side you reject. Second, the bodies are unbalanced in the wrong direction: 119 words for the view you reject against 88 for the view you hold, and the shorter one is the weaker (`contexts/06-feedback-patterns/model-feedback-examples.md` Example 1 shows a Tier-1 examiner capping TR at 6 for precisely this).
