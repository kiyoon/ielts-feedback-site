## Structural feedback

Body 1 has a recognisable point–explanation–example–assessment sequence, and its final “However” sentence is an acceptable evaluation link; the missing step is explaining how public funding enables the stated social benefit. The conclusion repeats that replacement claim instead of weighing art’s benefit against technology’s claimed reach or efficiency.
