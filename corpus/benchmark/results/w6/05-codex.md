## Structural feedback

Its missing step is policy relevance: explaining how public grants make the stated benefit possible. Body 2 starts with the relevant point that budgets are finite, but the chatbot example needs a direct cost or reach comparison before it can support the absolute claim that artists “can be replaced.” The conclusion’s “while” clause does weigh both sides, but the final sentence then repeats the waste verdict without qualification. The most valuable structural repair is to make both bodies directly compare uses of public funding; however, eliminating your recurring LR/GRA errors remains the fastest route from overall 6.5 to 7.0.
