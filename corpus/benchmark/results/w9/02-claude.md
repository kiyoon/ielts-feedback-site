## Structural feedback
The four-paragraph shape is right, the concession-then-rebuttal move in Body 1 is genuinely sophisticated, and your position is visible from the first paragraph. **The single highest-impact fix here is rewriting Body 2 so it argues about the data exchange, not about technology in general — that one repair is worth more than every lexical upgrade in the table below.** The prompt's trade-off is: *you hand over personal data, you get access to software.* Body 1 engages that trade-off. Body 2 does not: it argues that AI is useful and cheap, which nobody disputes and which would be equally true if the software were free and collected nothing. `contexts/05-band-6-to-7/task-response-upgrades.md` §3 calls this the scope test — if the paragraph would survive unchanged under a different prompt, it is not answering this one (`contexts/05-band-6-to-7/band-6-common-errors.md` §D4; `contexts/08-vocabulary-grammar/04-task-response-error-correction-bank.md` §3 row 2). The fix is one clause, not a new paragraph: the reason those AI services cost you nothing at the point of use *is* the data you hand over — that sentence converts the paragraph from off-axis to on-prompt.

Two smaller repairs. Your conclusion changes the question: the introduction promises "the benefits outweigh the [dra]wbacks", but the conclusion decides that "not utilizing modern technology is a more significant downside" — a comparison between *using and not using technology*, not between the advantages and disadvantages of the exchange. An "outweigh" prompt requires the verdict to be on the prompt's own terms (`contexts/04-task2-essays/question-types-overview.md` §3b; `contexts/08-vocabulary-grammar/04-task-response-error-correction-bank.md` §1 row 4). And Body 1's rebuttal — "there is always a risk to be stolen even if individuals choose not to use the service" — is a real argument, but it proves the *disadvantage is unavoidable*, not that the *advantage is larger*. Add one clause explaining why that makes the exchange worth accepting and it starts doing the weighing work.
