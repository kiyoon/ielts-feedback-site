## Prompt

Some people think that parents should teach their children how to be good members of society. Others, however, believe that school is the best place to learn this. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## Essay

Some would argued that parents are responsible to teach their children how to socialize and become good members of society, while others believe that school is the best place to handle that issue. Although there are homeroom teachers who can educate students how to socialize, I believe that parents are more responsible to their children.

On the one hand, children can learn how to socialize in school effectively thanks to homeroom teachers. This is because teachers are experts who has cirtification to teach juveniles. For instance, in Korea, they need to graduate the school of education and get a bachlor degree, and pass the exam to become an official teacher who can finally work in public schools. Therefore, it is often true that they can teach and guide children how to be a good member of society better than their parents. However, I believe that teachers cannot efficiently teach every children how to socialize because even though they are experts, they tend to get overwhelmed by the number of students they have to teach.

On the other hand, parents know their children more than anyone else, thus, they should know how to effectively guide their children. It is undeniable that they had observed their children for a long time, more than homeroom teachers. Moreover, everyone has different traits and personalities. Some might have mental health issues or even a rare disease. I am inclined to this view because, as a homeroom teacher, it is challenging to deal with these factors since there are so many children they have to take care of.

In conclusion, despite the fact that teachers in schools are experts to guide children and educate how to socialize with others and become nice adults, it is easy to get outnumbered by children.
